# Contributing

Full guide: [`docs/contributing.md`](docs/contributing.md) (English) —
[`docs/fr/contributing.md`](docs/fr/contributing.md) (français).

**Quick summary:**

- Real-world feedback on a runbook → open a **Feedback** issue.
- A new incident scenario worth a runbook → open a **New scenario** issue.
- A missing or incorrect translation → open a **Missing translation** issue.
- Runbook pull requests ship English + French together, every technical
  action pairs a dedicated tool with a free/open-source alternative, and no
  runbook names a legal deadline or a government agency in its body.
