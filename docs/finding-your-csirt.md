# Finding Your CSIRT

Every runbook in this project tells you, at the notification step, to escalate
internally first — your leadership and legal/DPO function then decide whether
and how to contact an external authority. This page is the pointer for that
step: a directory of directories, to help your organization find the right
national or regional CERT/CSIRT quickly, without digging through search
results in the middle of an incident.

**One term, two meanings.** "CSIRT" and "CERT" can mean either your own
organization's internal incident response team, or an external
national/governmental body that coordinates response at a country level and
may be the one to receive breach notifications. This page is about the
second kind — external, national or regional bodies — not your own internal
team.

## Global directory

- **[FIRST Teams Directory](https://www.first.org/members/teams/)** — the
  contact directory for incident response teams participating in FIRST
  (Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams), searchable by team name
  or country code. Start here first.

## Regional directories

- **[AfricaCERT](https://www.africacert.org/)** — pan-African organization
  coordinating computer emergency response across the continent.
- **[CSIRTAmericas](https://www.csirtamericas.org/)** — network of
  governmental CSIRTs from OAS member states, covering the Americas.
- **[APCERT](https://www.apcert.org/)** — cooperation framework for CSIRTs in
  the Asia Pacific region.
- **[TF-CSIRT / Trusted Introducer](https://www.trusted-introducer.org/)** —
  directory and trust framework for incident response teams, with a European
  focus.

## How to use this page

1. Search the FIRST Teams Directory for your country first.
2. If your national team isn't listed there, check the regional directory
   for your continent.
3. Still can't identify who to contact? Your data protection authority or
   your sector's regulator (finance, healthcare, telecom, critical
   infrastructure, etc.) can usually point you to the right CERT/CSIRT.

## What this page is not

This is a directory of contacts — not legal advice. It doesn't tell you
whether you're legally required to notify anyone, or within what timeframe:
that depends on your jurisdiction, your sector, and the nature of the
incident. Consult your legal counsel.
